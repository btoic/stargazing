# Messier Catalog Difficulty Ratings

Reference ratings excerpted from *The NexStar User Guide II* by Michael Swanson ([NexStar Resource Site](https://www.nexstarsite.com/OddsNEnds/MessierDifficultyRatings.htm)).

> **Observing Condition Standard**: Ratings assume good dark-sky transparency without moonlight, with objects observed near meridian transit. Northern observers will experience higher difficulty with low-declination objects (e.g. Sagittarius/Scorpius).

### Difficulty Rating Tiers
- **Easy**: Readily located in finder scope or wide-field eyepiece from suburban/dark sites; distinct features noticeable.
- **Medium**: Requires systematic star hopping; visible in moderate apertures, but detail demands dark skies or averted vision.
- **Hard**: Faint, small, or low-contrast surface brightness; requires dark skies (Bortle 4 or better), careful hop verification, or specialized filters.
