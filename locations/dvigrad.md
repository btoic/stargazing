## 2. Light Pollution Profile
*(Source: [lightpollutionmap.info](https://www.lightpollutionmap.info) & VIIRS / World Atlas)*

- **Bortle Class**: **Class 4** (Rural / Suburban Transition)
- **Zenith Sky Brightness (SQM)**:
  - World Atlas 2015: **20.86 mag/arcsec²**
  - Zenith Model Trend: **21.15 mag/arcsec²**
- **Artificial Brightness**: 203 – 316 µcd/m²
- **Ratio to Natural Sky Background**: 1.19 – 1.85
- **Naked-Eye Limiting Magnitude (NELM)**: ~6.0 – 6.2 mag overhead
- **Sky Character**:
  - The summer/autumn Milky Way is distinctly structured from Sagittarius / Scutum through Cygnus and Cassiopeia with prominent dark dust rifts (Great Rift).
  - Coastal light domes from Rovinj (to the SW, ~15 km), Poreč (to the W/NW, ~22 km), and Pula (to the S, ~30 km) remain low on the horizon, largely shielded by the surrounding karst terrain.
  - Superb sky darkness and high contrast overhead and toward the East, Southeast, and Zenith.

---

## 3. Horizon & Terrain Obstruction
- **Topography**: Deep limestone valley depression (Draga) bordered by oak and pine forest ridges.
- **Horizon Cutoff**: Creates a **10° to 15° obstruction** above the true astronomical horizon:
  - **South / Southwest**: 12°–15° cutoff (Southern targets like Sagittarius core sink early into the trees).
  - **East / Northeast**: 10°–14° cutoff (Rising targets like Saturn or Perseus clear the trees around 15°).
  - **Zenith (>30°)**: Completely unobstructed, crystalline transparency.
- **Practical Observing Rule**: Targets must be at **altitude ≥ 15°** to be visible, preferably **≥ 20°** to clear boundary-layer thermal turbulence and low-altitude atmospheric extinction.

---

## 4. Local Microclimate & Field Craft
- **Valley Dew**: Nocturnal cold air drains into the Draga basin, concentrating moisture. Dew shields, warm eyepiece storage in coat pockets, and keeping dust covers on during breaks are essential.
- **Wind Protection**: The valley provides natural shielding from strong coastal winds.
